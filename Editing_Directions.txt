**Global Text UI Style Guide (Zero White Text, No Extra Sounds)**

* **Font:** Bold Rounded Sans-Serif (*Noto Sans Telugu Bold* + *Montserrat ExtraBold*).
* **Text Colors:** **Deep Navy Blue (`#1B2A4A`)** for primary text; **Warm Terracotta Orange (`#C84B28`)** for myth-busting/alerts. Never use white text.
* **UI Box Style:** Rounded pill/card (`Corner Radius: 16px`), **3px solid `#1B2A4A` border**, and a **hard flat drop shadow** (`X: 5px, Y: 5px, Blur: 0px, Color: #1B2A4A`).
